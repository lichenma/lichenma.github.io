    How the bot chooses

    The bot favors damage, accuracy, same-type moves, recovery when hurt, and useful setup. It occasionally switches out of a bad spot and never reads your choice before making its own.

    Local battle engine

    This single file runs the full Pokémon battle simulation right in your browser using the same engine Pokémon Showdown uses. Random teams are dealt locally and every turn resolves on your device; the dojo bot picks its moves with a simple built-in heuristic.

    • No account, room list, ladder, cookies, or saved history.
    • Nothing is stored after you close the tab.
    • No battle server connection — it works offline once the page has loaded.

    Battle sprites load from Pokémon Showdown's public sprite archive. Everything else — teams, damage, AI — happens locally.
