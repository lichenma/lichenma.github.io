Gloom Garden

A tiny home-cooked wave survival. Pop the glooms, guard the garden.

Move: WASD or arrow keys · Aim: mouse · Fire: hold click or SPACE (gamepad: left stick move, right stick aim, RT/A fire)
Glooms waddle in from the edges — two pops each, and the big ones split into two little ones. Five hearts, so keep some distance. R restarts.
