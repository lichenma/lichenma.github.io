Smash Light

A tiny home-cooked platform fighter. Rack up %, launch them past the blast zone.

You are Ember (press 2 + R to play as Shade instead)  ·  A/D move · W jump (×2) · S fast-fall / drop · J attack · K special · L shield · R restart
Gamepad: stick / D-pad move · A jump · X attack · B special · RB shield
