Ricochet Rumble

A tiny home-cooked maze duel. Shots bounce — mind the ricochet.

Pick your fighter: 1 = Crow Mage · 2 = Frost Admiral · 3 = Hornet Captain
Move: WASD or arrow keys · Aim: mouse · Fire: click or SPACE (gamepad: left stick move, right stick aim, RT/A fire)
Shots ricochet off walls up to 4 times. Three hearts a round, first to win 3 rounds takes the match. R restarts.
