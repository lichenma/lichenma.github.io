Games

Pokémon Showdown Bot Dojo

A chill, low-effort Pokémon battle sim that runs entirely in your browser. Pick a generation, get a random team, and battle a heuristic bot — no team building, no grinding, no login.

  • Random battles: Generations 4 through 9 singles, plus Gen 9 Random Doubles
  • Heuristic bot opponent (type matchups, smart switching)
  • Critical-hit flashes, held items, speed ranges, full stats on hover
  • Battles run live on Pokémon Showdown's public server — internet required
Enter the Dojo

Smash Light

A tiny home-cooked platform fighter that runs entirely in your browser. Rack up %, launch them past the blast zone, and be the last one standing — simple, chill, and snackable.

Ricochet Rumble

A tiny home-cooked maze duel that runs entirely in your browser. Pick an anime-flair shooter — black-flame orbs, slowing ice balls, or a two-hit butterfly stinger — and bank your shots off the walls to take the round.

Gloom Garden

A tiny home-cooked wave survival that runs entirely in your browser. Guard a cheerful garden from waddling glooms — pop them into sparkles, snatch the odd heart, and see how many waves you can hold.
